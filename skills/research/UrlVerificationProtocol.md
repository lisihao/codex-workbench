# URL verification protocol

Use a URL as evidence only after opening the target and confirming that its content supports the associated claim.

## Source order

1. Specifications, official documentation, source repositories, release notes, and original research papers.
2. Maintainer statements or first-party issue trackers when primary documentation is incomplete.
3. Independent secondary analysis only for context, disagreement, or operational experience.

Do not cite a search-results page, generated summary, mirrored page, or URL whose relevant content could not be read. For dynamic claims, record the observation date and the exact version, release, or commit when available.

## Claim ledger

For every material conclusion, retain:

- the precise claim;
- the direct URL;
- source type and publication or release date when available;
- the specific evidence found at that URL;
- whether the conclusion is a source fact, an engineering inference, or unresolved.

When sources disagree, report the disagreement and the decision consequence. Do not average incompatible measurements or convert missing evidence into a zero value.

Keep quotations short. Prefer a paraphrase linked to the supporting primary source.
