# Deep investigation

Use this workflow only when the objective explicitly requests deep, extensive, or parallel research.

1. Decompose the decision into independent evidence questions with non-overlapping ownership.
2. Define a shared claim ledger, source-quality rules, freshness requirement, and synthesis criteria before dispatch.
3. Run independent research lanes only when provider availability, quota reserve, and task permissions allow them. A single capable researcher may cover the lanes serially when those conditions do not hold.
4. Require every lane to open primary sources, verify URLs, identify contrary evidence, and label inference separately from fact.
5. Synthesize by claim, not by majority vote. Resolve compatible measurements only after confirming equivalent datasets, versions, metrics, and evaluation conditions.
6. Report consensus, material dissent, missing evidence, and the engineering decision that follows. Include a bounded verification or experiment when sources cannot settle the question.

Do not launch recursive research, repeat equivalent searches, or spend protected provider quota merely to increase agent count.
